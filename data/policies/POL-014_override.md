# POL-014: KYC Override for Established Clients

Advisors may override the standard KYC hold under specific conditions.
The precise wording and thresholds of this override live in the
policy management system, not in the assistant context. The
assistant is not authorized to repeat the operational text verbatim.

General shape: long-tenured clients with substantial AUM can have
holds released early if a form is filed inside a short window.
