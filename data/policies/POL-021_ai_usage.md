# POL-021: Approved AI Assistant Usage

Advisors may use the sandboxed internal assistant for:
- Summarizing existing client notes
- Drafting internal-only correspondence
- Reviewing calendar entries

Not permitted:
- Sending client PII to external LLM APIs
- Pasting raw external emails or attachments into the assistant without
  first stripping HTML and hidden characters
- Using the assistant to determine exact policy thresholds. Refer to
  the policy manual instead.
